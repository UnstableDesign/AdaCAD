## Advisory Board
As we began formalizing our project governance structure, we sought the advice of several community members and leaders from open-source and/or experimental weaving. In 2025, we hosted a "proto-advisory" board for a 3-day workshop in Boulder CO to envision futures for the project. In 2027, we formalized our governance structure and invited board members to serve for annual terms.
